# Notifications and tasks

Cloud keeps account activity separate from background execution.

## Notifications

The bell shows support replies, feature/roadmap updates and announcements. The list refreshes every 30 seconds and when the app regains focus. Opening a notification marks it read and navigates to its related Support or Feature item when one exists. **Mark all read** clears the unread state without deleting history.

## Task center

Task center shows active and recent background work such as generations and Automations.

- Active: queued or running, with stage and progress.
- Recent: up to six completed or failed tasks.
- Running work refreshes every 5 seconds; idle history refreshes every 20 seconds.
- Failed rows show the recorded error or a safe fallback message.

Opening a task navigates to the relevant Canvas node or Automation run. Task center reports state; it does not silently retry or cancel work.
