# Affiliate program

The affiliate program is available for **Scenelith Cloud**. Self-hosted instances do not include Scenelith billing or the partner program.

Scenelith Partners is managed through Rekomi. The current program pays **30% recurring commission** on eligible subscription payments for as long as the referred customer keeps an active paid subscription. A valid referral visit has a **30-day attribution window** and the most recent valid affiliate referral receives credit.

Program terms can change. The terms displayed when you join and inside your partner dashboard are the final source for a particular referral, commission or payout.

## Join the program

1. Open the [Scenelith affiliate page](https://scenelith.com/affiliates).
2. Select **Join the program** to open the Scenelith Partners portal.
3. Sign in to Rekomi or create an affiliate account and complete the enrollment form.
4. Open Scenelith Partners in the dashboard and copy your assigned tracked link.

The program is configured for automatic approval. Rekomi may still call the enrollment screen an application and ask for your name, country and promotion plan; submitting that form does not require a separate manual Scenelith review.

## Share your tracked link

Use the complete link shown in your partner dashboard. It contains the referral identifier needed to attribute a visit. You can link to the Scenelith homepage or another Scenelith marketing page as long as the assigned referral parameter remains intact.

For the most reliable attribution:

- do not remove or rewrite the referral parameter;
- send visitors to a Scenelith page before checkout;
- avoid URL shorteners or redirect services that discard query parameters;
- ask the customer to complete checkout in the same browser within 30 days;
- make accurate product, pricing and earnings claims.

Browser privacy settings, blockers or a redirect that removes the referral identifier can prevent a visit from being attributed.

## How commission is recorded

Scenelith carries the referral into Dodo Payments checkout metadata. Attribution follows the checkout order, product and subscription identifiers rather than matching a displayed price or currency.

An eligible first subscription payment creates commission for the attributed affiliate. Eligible renewals continue adding commission while that subscription remains active. A refund, dispute or reversed payment can remove the related commission.

The public program currently promises commission on eligible subscription payments. Do not assume that one-time credit packs or another purchase type is commissionable unless it is explicitly listed in the current program terms.

## Reporting and payouts

Use the Rekomi partner dashboard to view:

- your tracked link;
- recorded clicks and referred customers;
- conversions and recurring commissions;
- pending and available balance;
- payout status and required payout or tax setup.

Reporting is not always immediate. If a paid subscription is still missing after processing, open [Support](./support.md) from the Scenelith product menu or email [support@scenelith.com](mailto:support@scenelith.com). Include your affiliate email, the approximate click and checkout time, and the customer or order reference. Do not send card details or passwords.

## Troubleshooting

**The program page asks me to apply.** Complete the Rekomi enrollment form. Scenelith Partners uses automatic approval even though the provider labels the action as an application.

**The customer opened my link but no click appears.** Check that the complete referral parameter is still present after redirects. Retry in a clean browser session without a blocker.

**A payment appears without commission.** Confirm that the customer followed your assigned link before checkout, purchased within the attribution window and did not follow a newer affiliate link. Allow time for payment processing before contacting support.

**A commission disappeared.** Check the conversion status in Rekomi. Refunds, disputes and reversed payments can reverse the corresponding commission.
