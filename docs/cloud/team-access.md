# Team access

Team access is a Scenelith Cloud Studio feature. Studio includes five total seats: the owner plus up to four teammates. The workspace shares one credit pool, while project/Canvas visibility is granted per member.

## Invite a teammate

Only an owner can invite. Choose an email that does not already have a Scenelith account, then select at least one Canvas. An invitation reserves a seat while pending and expires after seven days.

The owner can grant Canvases from workspaces they own, up to 250 Canvas grants in one invitation/member selection. A teammate sees only those exact Canvases and their associated project resources.

If Cloud email delivery fails, the invitation stays pending and the API reports that it was saved but not delivered; the owner can retry from the pending list. Resending rotates the link, extends expiry and is limited to ten send attempts with at least one minute between successful send attempts.

## Owner controls

The owner can:

- change the Canvas selection for a pending invitation;
- resend or revoke a pending invitation;
- change an existing member's access;
- remove an eligible team-managed member account from the team;
- see seat use and pending invitations.

Pending invitations count toward the seat limit. A new invitation is blocked when all seats are active or reserved.

## Member privacy

A member sees the workspace owner, their own account and their assigned Canvas access. The total team size and other members stay private. Members cannot manage invitations or grants.
